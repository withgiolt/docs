# GlowVM is retired

GlowVM ran Gleam's Erlang target on AtomVM compiled to WebAssembly. It's archived and gets no
further releases. Giolt builds Erlang-target apps with afterglow instead, which compiles your app's
BEAM bytecode to WebAssembly ahead of time.

See [About afterglow](/afterglow/about) and [How to use afterglow](/afterglow/how-to-use).
